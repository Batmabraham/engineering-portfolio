## What each project will include

- A clear problem statement and measurable requirements.
- Alternatives and the reasoning behind design decisions.
- Calculations, inputs, units, assumptions, and instructions for reproducing results.
- Drawings and figures that explain the structure or system.
- Verification, limitations, and conclusions supported by the work.

Additional class and independent projects will be added as they develop. Completed results will be distinguished from preliminary work.

## About the work

These are educational projects. Each project documents its scope and limitations; the pipe-support study is a conceptual design, not a construction-ready or nuclear-qualified component.

AI assistance is used for guidance, documentation, and review. Any assisted implementation or analysis should be identified in the relevant project, with checks recorded before results are treated as verified.
